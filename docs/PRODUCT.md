# Current product boundaries

Arabic-first Tawjihi learning for Palestinian students. This records product decisions; current user instructions take precedence. The published catalog lives in `src/data/course.js`. Earlier conflicting plans remain in Git history and do not authorize new features.

## Entry and access

The app has one learner experience. More opens a blank page. Unfinished navigation tabs show a brief bottom message (`قريبًا`) while preserving the current tab, view, URL and lesson progress. Retired developer URLs return to Home.

- Choose curriculum (Gaza/Palestine) and path (Scientific/Literary), then enter the shared subjects Home at `?page=learn`.
- An account is required to access Home, subjects and lessons. Signed-out visitors see Create account/Sign in; there is no guest trial. Direct learning links require authentication, and sign-out returns to entry. Registration requires username, curriculum, path, phone, password one step at a time, ending at password. The phone field shows `0591234567` as its placeholder. Local `059`/`056` numbers require exactly ten digits; international numbers require a country code and valid country-specific numbering. Format guidance appears only with validation errors. Registration never collects email. Prototype registration is immediate; no verification-code detour or language selector.
- Sign in offers username/phone plus password; existing email identifiers remain compatible with saved accounts. Successful actions return to the same Home.
- Student account types are `guest`, `free`, `subscribed`, `banned`; staff roles are separate. Banned students reach Home with subject access blocked.
- Preserve vibrant cards, subtle gradients, white filled-action labels, and original gem/heart/level icons. Member statistics derive from confirmed progress. Free students start with 200 gems and 10 hearts. Every submitted answer, right or wrong, costs one heart; one heart refills every 12 minutes, up to 10 (two hours from empty). Subscribed Super/Pro students have unlimited hearts and gems, displayed as ∞. HTTP balances are stored per account and spending is atomic with answer progress; fixture balances are saved locally.
- At zero hearts, the lesson fades into a full-screen pause with sad Rocky holding a heart in both hands and a live countdown to the next free heart. Students can return to the lesson or activate their one-time free week of Super, offered with unlimited hearts and detailed lesson explanations. Activation grants seven days of unlimited resources, expires automatically without billing, and never changes the paid account type. The server retains the trial expiry per account across devices; fixture mode retains it locally. A refill enables Continue in place. Reduced motion shows the screen immediately; dismissal or navigation cleans up the dialog and countdown while preserving question progress.
- Eligible free accounts also see a small offer at the left of Home's greeting, with Rocky in sunglasses and an «أسبوع مجاني» tag underneath. A soft pulse, breathing motion and tag shimmer make it visibly clickable. It opens the same free-week offer and disappears after the trial is claimed; subscribed accounts do not see it. The offer and activation use the existing production Rocky SVG, breathing animation, glasses and heart artwork with the app's shared palette and controls. Active Super shows floating hearts and a white glasses shine. Reduced motion keeps these still. The active free week's countdown appears on Home. The question filter is retained behind `QUESTION_FILTER_ENABLED = false`; question difficulty ratings keep their controls without the introductory prompt.
- New accounts remain on Rocky’s success celebration until Continue opens Home. The first-account tour is retained but disabled by `ROCKY_TOUR_ENABLED` in `src/main.js`; keep its account-scoped state and demo behavior intact.


## Learning

The ICT student course is **questions only**, with five textbook lessons grouped under the book's three units. Each lesson opens one continuous question collection, ordered by the retained book-topic parts. The roadmap shows one circular question-progress indicator per lesson. Videos, summaries and the historical introduction are absent from the student journey. Original source files, published IDs and account records are retained. `src/data/subject-roadmaps.js` owns order and legacy part identities; [ICT_COVERAGE.md](ICT_COVERAGE.md) records question sources and exclusions. The supplied 2024 Gaza textbook defines scope.

Keep completion, mastery, review, XP and access distinct. Published lesson/part/step IDs are durable identities. Unpublished parts show an honest preparation state. Retired tooling cannot complete a learner's part or award XP.

The current question review records repeated MCQ/true-false mistakes and returns the student to the preceding explanation and specific question. Correct review clears that item; interruption preserves it. Each current question earns XP only on its first correct answer; wrong answers, repeats and lesson completion add none. Home shows levels 1–500, with 100 XP per level, using the existing bolt artwork. Starting at level 1, the 499 increases require exactly 49,900 XP. The shared reward catalog divides this integer pool evenly across unique current question IDs; the final unsolved question is required to reach level 500. Duplicate placements, legacy links and Mathematics 1/2 aliases share a reward. Hidden examples and retired questions earn none. The first answered question of each local day, right or wrong, increases the account's streak and briefly shows its celebration. Written cards require revealing the solution and choosing right (green) or wrong (red). The choice can be changed until Continue saves it; selected buttons darken slightly, and Continue with neither selected prompts for a choice without advancing. Only a confirmed right answer earns the first-answer reward. Historical unassessed card reviews still count as activity without XP. Saved correct-answer flags are repriced under the shared scale while question identities, completion, mistakes and historical activity remain saved. `src/domain/subject-progress.js` owns reward and activity rules; `src/domain/progression.js` owns the level scale.

MCQs use selectable options; written questions use flip cards with sourced answers and explicit right/wrong self-checks. Long text and images scroll inside the cards. A question fills its lesson ring only after a correct answer; historical completion flags cannot complete newly added questions. Counts come from the active bank/index, not duplicated documentation. Original choices, diagrams and lesson/part/question identities remain stable.

Ordinary question layouts omit Rocky. Source diagrams enlarge with keyboard-accessible controls. Answers distinguish checked source keys, corrected source mistakes and authored solutions. The disabled first-account demo selects existing canonical questions without changing published order or completing an entire lesson; preserve its source mappings and saved account state.

The lesson close button ends the current study session with Rocky’s existing celebration, session XP, first-attempt accuracy (including written self checks) and active learning time. Students can continue from that question or return to the roadmap; stopping early does not complete unanswered questions. The three statistics boxes rise into place in a short stagger, then their colored label bands open above stationary white faces, following the Rocky launch film’s component motion without its camera zoom. The same glossy yellow level track as Home fills with one SVG bolt per earned XP emitted from the statistics box’s main bolt after the statistics appear. The box’s number decreases for each departing bolt; larger rewards take longer, and level crossings use the shared level rules. On crossing a level, a golden badge springs in beside Rocky with a short burst of existing bolt SVGs and an accessible announcement. Further crossings update that badge smoothly. The spring integrator is adapted from Hairline under its MIT license; the app retains its own artwork and filled UI style. The animation never writes rewards and reduced motion shows final values immediately.

Every study question has a report flag at the left of its source-tag row, below the progress bar; the close button remains at the top left. Authenticated reports identify the published question, issue category and optional details. The flag also offers personal question hiding with an explicit warning and confirmation; Cancel receives initial focus. Hidden questions stay out of that student's lessons, mistake reviews and question filters across reloads and devices. Other students and published content are unaffected, and hiding spends no heart, grants no XP and does not answer or complete the question. Optional easy/medium/hard ratings are saved per learner and restored when revisiting the question. Rocky recommends rating as a way to identify mastered questions and review needs; Continue never requires a rating. Level gifts remain labelled as coming soon; hearts and gems use the account balances described above.

## Routes

| URL | Owner/behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `/`, `?flow=entry`, `?flow=register`, `?flow=sign-in` | Entry/account flows |
| `?page=learn` | Shared subjects Home |
| `?subject=ict` | ICT roadmap |
| `?subject=mathematics` | Mathematics 1 question roadmap |
| `?subject=ict&lesson=database-management&part=access-basics` | Full question lesson, resuming saved progress; legacy part identity remains valid |
| `?page=quests`, `shop`, `challenges`, `levels` | Locked; direct links normalize to Home |
| `?page=more` | Blank page |
| `?view=ui-lab`, `?view=design-system`, `?view=ship-ready-*` | Retired; normalize to Home |

`src/app/route.js` normalizes invalid routes. URLs never override publication, account or sequence gates. Answers save without adding history entries; Back/Forward and Exit restore the correct learner and roadmap. Review/completion are in-view states; a refresh restores saved part progress.

## Unresolved release work

- The project is not a completed production product. Paid access, payment processing, content operations, full assessments and broader provisional stories require explicit product scope; do not infer implementation approval from old plans.
- Preserve the selected bright palette while reporting its known contrast failures. The opt-in accessibility suite reports those contrast exceptions and fails on other WCAG violations. Do not expand exceptions or describe the palette as fully accessible.
- Production accounts use Cloudflare Workers with D1/Durable Objects; SQLite is the isolated local test backend. Hosting and recovery procedures belong in [OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md). Do not treat local tests as evidence of production capacity or recovery readiness.
- Fixture previews are browser-local test accounts. Production uses the Cloudflare account service. Downloaded public lessons can be cached; sign-in, uncached content and account synchronization still need a connection.
